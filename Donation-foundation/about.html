About Us

Our mission is to make the world a better place through generosity and support to those in need. Join us in our journey to help communities thrive.

ABOUT DONATION FOUNDATION

Since 2000, we have been dedicated to improving the lives of children. We have directly supported more than 50,000 children. Based on the 5 proven aspects of well-being that must be met for children to thrive, we developed a methodology and an app that measures and supports children in these areas. We work with local and on-the-ground partners to support the social workforce to ensure children thrive in families. Our vision is A Family for Every Child in Our Lifetime. We are a group of committed optimists who believe all children have the right to grow up in a caring environment. Our deep belief in collaboration has led us to partner with government entities, NGOs, and people with lived experience. Learn more about our global and local initiatives.

Our Mission

We are a non-profit organization dedicated to providing support to underserved communities around the world. Our mission is to improve lives by providing essential resources in the areas of education, healthcare, food security, and economic development. We believe in the power of collective action and the impact of small acts of kindness.

Our Vision

Our vision is a world where every individual has access to the resources they need to live a healthy, fulfilling life. We aim to be a global leader in charitable giving, creating lasting positive change in the lives of the most vulnerable populations.

Our Core Values

Compassion

We show empathy and kindness in every action, understanding that small gestures can create big changes in the lives of others.

Integrity

We operate with transparency, accountability, and ethical practices in every aspect of our work.

Collaboration

We believe in the power of partnerships and working together to solve the complex challenges facing our communities.
